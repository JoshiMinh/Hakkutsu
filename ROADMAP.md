# Hakkutsu Feature Roadmap

- [ ]  **Subtitle Immersion Hotkeys & Focus Modes:** Adds dedicated video playback hotkeys (`A`/`D` for cue skipping, `S` for instant cue replay, `R` for A-B sentence looping, `E` for toggling subtitle blur to encourage active listening) alongside an auto-pause mode at the end of each subtitle line for reading and mining.
- [ ]  **Cloze Deletion SRS Mode:** Automatically generates fill-in-the-blank cards from mined context sentences during SRS reviews (`今日は［……］を食べた`), training contextual recall alongside standalone word definitions.
- [ ]  **Audio-First SRS Review Mode:** Plays Japanese TTS or video audio clips prior to revealing the card text, testing auditory comprehension and pitch recognition before flipping the answer.
- [ ]  **Interactive Kanji Stroke Writing Quiz:** Integrates `HanziWriter`'s canvas quiz API (`writer.quiz()`) into SRS card reviews, allowing learners to practice drawing kanji stroke-by-stroke with real-time stroke-order validation.
- [ ]  **Morphological De-inflection Tree:** Implements a client-side de-inflector engine in definition popups to deconstruct complex conjugated verbs and adjectives into their root lemmas and affix chains (e.g., `食べさせられた` $\rightarrow$ `食べる` + causative + passive + past).
- [ ]  **Smart Decks & Leech Management:** Adds customizable filtered SRS review sessions by JLPT level (`N5`–`N1`), source domain, or custom tags, with automated leech detection flagging cards failed $\ge 4$ times.
- [ ]  **Synchronized Subtitle Script Drawer ("Reader Mode for Video"):** Provides a collapsible transcript sidebar on YouTube, Netflix, and HTML5 video players featuring auto-scrolling synced to video playback, full-text dialogue search, 1-click cue seeking, and real-time highlighting of known vs. unlearned vocabulary.
- [ ]  **Client-Side Video Audio Slicing & Clipping:** Extracts the exact audio segment of a subtitle cue in-memory during sentence mining using `AudioContext` and `MediaRecorder`, saving audio blobs into IndexedDB for local SRS playback and Anki `[sound:...]` export.
- [ ]  **FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) Engine:** Integrates the modern FSRS-4.5/FSRS-5 scheduling algorithm directly into `local-srs.ts` in pure TypeScript, computing optimal memory retention intervals and reducing daily review volume compared to SM-2.
- [ ]  **Selective Furigana & Webpage Immersion Density:** Offloads page text scanning to a background Web Worker to display a Japanese difficulty breakdown badge (e.g., *78% N3, 14% N2*) while selectively injecting `<ruby>` furigana into the DOM only for unlearned or high-level kanji.
- [ ]  **Offline Yomitan / Yomichan Dictionary Importer (`.zip`):** Enables drag-and-drop importing and client-side extraction (`DecompressionStream`/`JSZip`) of standard community dictionary `.zip` archives directly into IndexedDB (`HakkutsuDictDB`), enabling 100% offline multi-dictionary lookup.
- [ ]  **Built-in Local Media Player ("Immersion Hub"):** Adds a standalone extension player view (`app.html#/player`) that loads local video files (`.mp4`, `.mkv`, `.webm`) and subtitle files (`.srt`, `.vtt`, `.ass`) via `URL.createObjectURL`, bringing full Hakkutsu mining and dictionary overlays to offline files.
- [ ]  **Client-Side Box OCR for Manga & Images:** Integrates lightweight WebAssembly OCR (`tesseract.js` / ONNX) to let users drag a bounding box over manga scans, canvas elements, and web comics to extract and parse Japanese text without external APIs.
- [ ]  **Local Immersion Analytics & Activity Heatmap:** Tracks daily characters read, video immersion time, and mining volume with a GitHub-style activity contribution calendar and retention metrics in the dashboard.
