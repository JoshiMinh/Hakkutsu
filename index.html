DICTIONARY

Inline Dictionary & Furigana

Hover or select text on any webpage for readings, definitions, JLPT levels, pitch accents, and kanji stroke order diagrams. Clean furigana annotations above kanji help you read comfortably.

YOUTUBE & NETFLIX

Dual Subtitles & Transcript

Interactive dual subtitles for YouTube and Netflix. Hover any subtitle word to pause and inspect definitions. The side-panel transcript lets you follow, search, and jump to any timestamp.

MANGA

On-Device Manga OCR

Read raw Japanese manga directly in your browser. Bundled Tesseract LSTM detects speech bubbles and vertical text on canvas without uploading images to any third-party server.

ANKI

1-Click Sentence Mining

Capture the word, surrounding context sentence, source URL, screenshot, and audio to your vocabulary collection in one click. Configurable field mappings export cleanly to local Anki decks.

FSRS-4.5 / 5

FSRS Spaced Repetition

Review cards directly in browser with the DSR memory model (Difficulty, Stability, Retrievability). Includes Audio-First listening mode and Cloze Deletion context recall.

BACKUPS

Data Ownership & Backups

Export standard CSV for interoperability or full JSON backups preserving SRS scheduling state. Restore your collection anytime without resetting learning progress.

Review what you find.
