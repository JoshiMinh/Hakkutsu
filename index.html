Japanese immersion toolkit

Learn the words you actually encounter.

Hakkutsu turns Japanese webpages and videos into an interactive study environment—lookup, subtitles, sentence mining, Anki export, and review in one extension.

Download a releaseBuild from source

Free and open source · Local vocabulary storage · No account required

One reading loop, end to end.

Stay in context while reading or watching. Save useful language when it appears, then review it later without rebuilding cards by hand.

01

Inline dictionary

Readings, definitions, JLPT level, pitch accent, and real-world frequency information directly on the page.

02

Interactive subtitles

Timed subtitle overlays for YouTube, Netflix, and compatible third-party HTML5 video players.

03

Sentence mining

Capture the word, surrounding sentence, source, image, and useful metadata with minimal interruption.

04

Built-in SRS

Review saved cards using local scheduling, due queues, retention statistics, and learning-state filters.

05

AnkiConnect

Map fields to your own note model and export into a local Anki deck when you want full control.

06

Portable data

Keep everything local, export interoperable CSV, or preserve full SRS state in a restorable JSON backup.

Built around the content.

Compact interfaces keep the Japanese text and video in focus while the learning tools remain one interaction away.

Your vocabulary stays yours.

Hakkutsu stores cards and review history in the browser. Backups include scheduling metadata, so restoring a collection does not reset learning progress.

Local by default

No account is required. Normal extension updates preserve the local IndexedDB vocabulary database.

Backup when it matters

Export a full JSON backup before uninstalling, clearing browser data, or moving to another browser profile.
