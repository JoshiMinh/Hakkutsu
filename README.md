## Overview

Hakkutsu (meaning "excavation" or "discovery") is a browser extension created for language learners who want to read and listen to Japanese content online. Instead of switching tabs, searching dictionary websites, or manually copying words into flashcard software, Hakkutsu brings dictionary lookup, readings, pitch accent details, and contextual sentence explanations directly into the web pages and videos you visit.

Whether you are reading news articles, light novels, social media posts, or watching video content with subtitles on platforms like YouTube and Netflix, Hakkutsu helps you understand unfamiliar vocabulary on the spot and save it for long-term retention.

---

## Why Use Hakkutsu?

- **Immerse Without Interruption**: Read native Japanese websites and watch videos while looking up words in place without breaking your focus.
- **Deep Contextual Understanding**: Get immediate dictionary entries, furigana readings, pitch accent indicators, and optional AI-assisted grammar breakdowns.
- **Effortless Flashcard Mining**: Save new vocabulary and source sentences to your local Anki desktop application with a single click, or use the built-in study review system.
- **Privacy-First Design**: Operates locally inside your web browser. Your data, saved words, and configuration settings stay on your machine.

---

## Key Features

### Instant Word and Sentence Lookup
Highlight text, double-click, or hover over Japanese words to open clean, detailed entry cards. Each lookup card includes:
- Japanese readings and furigana annotations.
- Clear definitions and part-of-speech labels.
- JLPT level indicators (N5 to N1) to help gauge word difficulty.
- Sino-Vietnamese (Hán-Việt) auxiliary readings for learners who study through Vietnamese.
- Audio pronunciation playback for vocabulary entries.

### AI-Assisted Sentence and Grammar Help
When sentences contain idiomatic expressions, complex grammar, or non-standard syntax, Hakkutsu provides sentence analysis:
- Step-by-step breakdown of sentence structure and particle usages.
- Context-aware translations tailored to the surrounding paragraph.

### Anki and Spaced Repetition Integration
- **AnkiConnect Export**: Connects to your local Anki program to export vocabulary cards complete with terms, readings, definitions, audio, and example sentences into your chosen deck.
- **Built-in SRS Dashboard**: If you do not use Anki, Hakkutsu provides an internal spaced repetition study system. Review saved cards directly within the extension dashboard at recommended review intervals.

### Video Subtitle Immersion
Study Japanese media on platforms like YouTube and Netflix:
- Interactive dual subtitles with clickable tokens.
- Hovering over subtitle words pauses video playback and displays instant definitions.
- Easily toggle primary and secondary subtitles on or off.

## Getting Started

### Installation Steps

1. Obtain the compiled extension folder (`chrome-mv3-prod`) from the `build` directory or release package.
2. Open Google Chrome (or any compatible Chromium-based browser such as Brave, Edge, or Vivaldi).
3. Navigate to `chrome://extensions` in your address bar.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** using the toggle in the upper-right corner.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `chrome-mv3-prod` directory (or drag and drop the extension zip file onto the page).
6. Pin the Hakkutsu icon to your browser toolbar for easy access.

### Initial Setup

1. Click the Hakkutsu extension icon in your toolbar to open the quick settings menu.
2. Choose your preferred word lookup shortcut (such as highlight text, double-click, or Alt + Hover).
3. If using Anki, ensure Anki is running with the AnkiConnect add-on installed, then choose your target deck in the settings page.

## Using the Learning Hub Dashboard

Click **Open Dashboard** from the popup or open the extension options to access the full-screen Learning Hub:
- **Dashboard Overview**: View daily learning statistics, total mined vocabulary counts, and study streaks.
- **Review**: Perform interactive spaced repetition card reviews.
- **Vocabulary**: Manage, search, filter, and organize your saved vocabulary list.
- **Settings**: Configure language options, lookup preferences, and Anki card templates.
