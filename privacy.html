Privacy Policy for Hakkutsu (発掘)

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Hakkutsu ("we", "our", or "the extension") is an AI-powered Japanese immersion browser extension designed to assist learners with reading Japanese text, inline dictionary lookups, offline screen OCR, and Anki card mining. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparency regarding data handling.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Hakkutsu does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII), including your name, email address, IP address, location, browsing history, or payment details. We run no telemetry or tracking analytics servers.

3. Local Data Storage & Processing

  • Dictionary & Tokenizer: Morphological analysis (Sudachi & Kuromoji) and dictionary lookups are processed 100% locally in your browser.
  • Offline Screenshot OCR: Screen crop image data is processed entirely on your local machine using Tesseract OCR Web Workers running inside a Chrome MV3 Offscreen Worker. No crop images are uploaded to any external server.
  • User Settings & SRS Cards: Your preference settings and saved vocabulary items are stored strictly on your device using local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

4. Optional External API Services

If you choose to enable AI sentence analysis or Anki synchronization:

  • Gemini / OpenAI API: Only the specific Japanese text snippet you request to analyze is sent directly to your configured AI provider using your personal API key.
  • AnkiConnect: Flashcard data is transferred directly to your local Anki application (`http://localhost:8765`).

5. Single Purpose & Policy Disclosures

Hakkutsu adheres strictly to Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies:

  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use user data for creditworthiness or lending purposes.

6. Contact & Open Support

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit ko-fi.com/joshiminh or contact the extension author on GitHub.
