Privacy policy

Your saved words and review history are stored in your browser. Hakkutsu has no user accounts or analytics tracking. Below is a guide to storage, permissions, and connections.

01 Overview

Hakkutsu helps you study Japanese webpages, subtitles and images. No Hakkutsu account is required. The extension contains no advertising or behavioral analytics. Online features send the text or queries needed for the requested feature to third-party providers; it is not a fully offline extension.

02 Stored data

Saved vocabulary, sentences, source URLs and titles, screenshots, audio, review history, Anki links and imported dictionaries are stored in browser storage or IndexedDB. Preferences use browser sync storage and may sync through your browser account. Anki data you explicitly sync is also stored in Anki; AnkiWeb synchronization is controlled by Anki.

03 External connections

Sentence and webpage translation send text to Google Translate. Online dictionary and example lookups send words or search queries to providers such as Jisho, Mazii and Tatoeba. Illustration searches send queries to Irasutoya and may translate search terms with Google. Pronunciation sends the text to Google speech services, with browser or operating-system speech as a fallback. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address and handle requests under their own policies. Subtitle features request captions from the video provider and may translate subtitle text. Requests support language study, not advertising or activity tracking.

04 Browser permissions

Page access and scripting enable lookup, furigana, subtitle interaction and image selection on supported webpages. Tabs and activeTab support communication with the current tab, opening extension pages and user-triggered screenshot capture. Storage saves settings and learning data. The sidePanel permission provides the transcript panel. Offscreen documents run packaged OCR processing in Chrome. Host access permits these page features, provider requests and the local AnkiConnect bridge. Browser-protected pages cannot run extension content scripts.

05 OCR and Anki

Image recognition uses packaged Tesseract code and language data on your device. Screenshot/image crops are processed locally for OCR and may be retained when you save a card. Import, export, sync and review requests to AnkiConnect use the local desktop endpoint on port 8765 and can include card text, media, tags, scheduling information and note identifiers. Hakkutsu does not operate an account or collection-sync server.

06 Export and deletion

You can export or back up your collection and remove saved entries using the extension. Clearing extension storage or uninstalling removes browser-local data; exported files, browser-synced preferences and data copied to Anki or its backups may remain until you remove them separately. The extension does not sell learning data or use it to determine creditworthiness. Provider requests are limited to the language-study features described above.

07 Verify and contact

You can inspect the open-source implementation and extension background/network activity to see storage and provider requests. Online translation, dictionary, illustration, speech and caption features are expected to make external requests. For privacy questions or bug reports, use the support link at https://github.com/JoshiMinh/Hakkutsu/issues. Avoid posting private card contents or credentials in public issues.
